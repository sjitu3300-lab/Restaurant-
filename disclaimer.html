Foodzilla Legal & Food Disclaimer

Important dietary warnings, allergen notices, nutritional value calculations and operational disclaimers.

1. Food Allergy & Cross-Contamination Notice

Important Dietary Notice: Foodzilla Fast Food kitchen me dairy products (pure cheese, butter, cream, malai paneer), gluten (wheat flour burger buns & pizza dough), soy sauce, sesame seeds aur mustard regular basis par use hote hain.

Hum strict kitchen hygiene aur separate preparation boards use karte hain, par severe food allergies wale customers ko advise kiya jata hai ki wo order dene se pehle staff ya manager se clarify karein.

2. Nutritional Values & Calorie Count Methodology

Website par show kiye gaye Calories (kcal), Protein, Carbs aur Fat values standard theoretical recipes ke base par approximate calculations hain.

Handcrafted preparation, manual portion sizing aur customer dwara custom extra cheese / sauces add karne par exact nutritional values vary ho sakti hain.

3. Food Freshness & Consumption Advisory

  • Instant Consumption: Fast food items (burgers, french fries, pizzas, momos) ko best crispiness aur taste ke liye deliver hone ke 30 se 45 minutes ke andar garam consume karna recommended hai.
  • Storage: Foodzilla kisi bhi food item ko late consume karne ya improper domestic storage ke karan taste badalne ke liye responsible nahi hoga.

4. Visual Assets & Third-Party Platforms

Website par use kiye gaye visual assets aur animations presentation purpose ke liye hain. Actual food presentation serving basket aur garnishing ke hisaab se minor vary kar sakti hai.

WhatsApp, Google Maps aur external payment gateways third-party services hain jinpar unki respective platform policies apply hoti hain.
