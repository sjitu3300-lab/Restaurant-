Legal & Food Disclaimer

Important informational, nutritional and allergy guidance for all valued customers.

1. Food Allergy & Cross-Contact Advisory

Important Health Notice: The Royal Dine kitchen me milk, cheese, paneer, wheat (gluten), mustard, sesame, and cashews (nuts) regularly use kiye jate hain. Hum strict sanitization aur separate utensils use karte hain, par absolute allergen-free environment ki 100% guarantee nahi di ja sakti.

Yadi aapko severe food allergy hai to kripya order dene se pehle hamare restaurant manager se direct clarify karein.

2. Nutritional & Calorie Count Estimates

Menu me show kiye gaye Calories (kcal), Protein, Carbs aur Fat values standard recipes aur theoretical calculations par based estimations hain.

Portion size variation, manual cooking technique, custom dressing/extra cheese add-ons ke karan actual nutritional values thodi different ho sakti hain.

3. Food Freshness & Consumption Advisory

  • Dine-In & Takeaway: Food ko best taste aur texture ke liye fresh aur hot serve hone par turant consume karna recommended hai.
  • Takeaway Storage: Takeaway food ko delivery ke baad 1–2 ghante ke andar consume karein ya proper refrigeration me rakhein. Hum late storage ke karan quality change ke liye responsible nahi hain.

4. Website Images & Third-Party Links

Website par use kiye gaye visual assets, food photography aur animations illustrative purposes ke liye hain. Actual food presentation serving bowl aur garnishing ke hisaab se vary kar sakti hai.

Website par diye gaye external links (jaise WhatsApp ya Google Maps) third-party platforms hain jinke policies par hamara direct control nahi hai.
