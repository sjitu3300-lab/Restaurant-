Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 | How we collect, safeguard and process your dining & booking information.

1. Introduction & Overview

The Royal Dine Restaurant ("Hum", "Hamara", ya "Restaurant") aapki privacy aur personal data ki security ko bohot seriously leta hai. Yeh Privacy Policy explain karti hai ki jab aap hamari website visit karte hain, table reserve karte hain, ya WhatsApp/phone se order place karte hain, to hum kaunsi information collect karte hain aur uska use kaise hota hai.

Hamari website use karke aap is policy me diye gaye terms aur data collection practices ko accept karte hain.

2. Information We Collect

Hum only wo zaroori details collect karte hain jo aapko best hospitality aur smooth order experience dene ke liye required hain:

  • Personal Details: Aapka Name, Contact Number (Mobile/WhatsApp), aur Email ID (agar form me provide kiya gaya ho).
  • Reservation Data: Date, Preferred Time, Guest Count, aur koi special dining preferences (e.g. birthday setup ya food allergies).
  • Technical & Device Info: Basic analytics jaise browser type, page visit timings, aur IP address taaki hum website performance improve kar sakein.

3. 4-Step Data Usage & Processing

Aapka data in 4 specific steps me use kiya jata hai:

  • Step 1 (Order & Booking Confirmation): Table booking verify karne aur order status WhatsApp/SMS par deliver karne ke liye.
  • Step 2 (Customer Support): Aapki inquiries, feedback aur delivery assistance ko quickly resolve karne ke liye.
  • Step 3 (Safety & Hygiene Compliance): Food safety standards aur government hospitality regulations ke compliance ke liye.
  • Step 4 (Offers & Updates): Agar aapne opt-in kiya hai, to special festival discounts aur seasonal menu updates share karne ke liye.

4. Data Protection & Third-Party Sharing

Hum aapki personal information ko kisi bhi third-party marketing agency ko kabhi sell ya rent nahi karte. Data sirf authorized restaurant management software aur direct communication tools (jaise WhatsApp Business API) par safely store hota hai.

Hum industry standard encryption aur secure servers use karte hain jisse aapka data unauthorized access se safe rahe.
