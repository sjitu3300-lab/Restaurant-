The Royal Dine website aur iske services (table reservation, digital menu, aur takeaway orders) ka upyog karke aap in Terms & Conditions se legally bound hone ke liye agree karte hain. Agar aap kisi bhi shart se sahmat nahi hain to kripya hamari online services use na karein.

2. Table Reservation & Holding Policy

  • Grace Period (15 Minutes): Reserved table aapke scheduled arrival time se maximum 15 minutes tak hold ki jayegi. Late aane par table available queue me release ho sakti hai.
  • Group & Party Bookings: 8 ya usse adhik logon ki party booking ke liye minimum 3 ghante pehle intimation dena zaroori hai.
  • Cancellation: Table cancel karne ke liye kripya kam se kam 1 ghanta pehle WhatsApp ya call dwara inform karein.

3. Pricing, Menu & Taxes

Website par show kiye gaye sabhi food items ke prices Indian Rupees (INR ₹) me hain. Applicable government taxes (GST) bill me according to rules shamil kiye jayenge.

Seasonal ingredients ki availability ke hisaab se menu items aur unke prices bina kisi purva notice ke update ho sakte hain.

4. Dietary Preferences & Allergies

The Royal Dine ek 100% Pure Vegetarian restaurant hai. Yadi aapko kisi specific ingredient (jaise peanuts, dairy, gluten, ya extra spices) se allergy hai, to order place karte waqt waiter ya chef ko pehle hi inform karna customer ki responsibility hai.

5. Code of Conduct & Governing Jurisdiction

Restaurant premises me family-friendly aur dignified environment maintain karna anivarya hai. Kisi bhi dispute ya legal claim ke case me jurisdiction exclusively Nagaur, Rajasthan Courts ke under aayega.
